A formula in a line is one box (GoTipe Atlas, frame 24, D-172): the fill under the pointer and the
selection of a formula being edited enclose all of it, a fraction's numerator and denominator
included — before, they were the strip of text, and a fraction stuck out of it. A line with a
formula keeps the height of the other lines: the formula's hidden text no longer pushes it lower.
TeX that cannot be read stays inline on its red tint.
